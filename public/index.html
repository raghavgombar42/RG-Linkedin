This bot catches the raw note, checks whether it's worth telling, adds a current angle if one fits, and drafts the post in my voice. Then it stops. I read it, edit it and post it myself.

01 · Why

Two weeks in, the pattern is already visible

I started posting in September 2026. The first post showed what happens when a real story gets written. The problem is everything that never gets that far.

4,981impressions on post #1, about 3.5× my follower count
163 · 30+likes · comments on that same post
3posts published in 2 weeks, 1,428 followers
2–3 / wkstories noticed, held in my head or phone notes, mostly lost

Pain · User · Outcome

Pain: Stories from Mesa sessions, BLUNNT operations and eyewear research fade before they get written. The ones that do get written take a long sitting to sound like me rather than like LinkedIn.

User: Me, Raghav Gombar. The only user.

Outcome: 3 publishable drafts a week, under 15 minutes of my time per post, and zero invented facts published under my name.

Journey today

  1. Something happens: a founder session, a Blue Dart run, a customer interview.
  2. I tell myself I'll write about it, and maybe jot one line in my Notes app.
  3. The week fills up: classes, BLUNNT orders, interviews for the eyewear idea.
  4. Days later I open a blank draft. The details have faded, and AI drafts sound like a guru.
  5. Only the stories I force through get posted. The rest disappear.
02 · How it works

Six steps, one human gate

A strict filter comes first, so the drafting model only sees notes with a real moment in them. The news hook is optional by design: my voice is story-first, so the news can support a post but never lead it.

Drop a note

Text or a voice note into my private Telegram channel. It takes 30 seconds, right when the thing happens.

Raghav · Trigger

Receive + transcribe

Telegram hands the message to the webhook. Voice notes are transcribed, Hinglish included.

Telegram → Vercel · Input

Score 0–10

A strict publishability check. Below 6 means no draft, just a one-line reason. To-dos and opinions with no story get filtered out.

Gemini Flash · Processing

Find a news angle

3–5 keywords → Google News → the top recent headline, source, date and link.

Google News RSS · Context

Draft in my voice

Note + Voice Skill + news. Missing details come back as [VERIFY: …], never guessed. Any news used carries a verify block.

Claude / Gemini · AI

I review and post

APPROVE or REJECT gets logged. I edit, read it aloud once and post it on LinkedIn myself. The bot never posts.

The Cut

Check 07 kills auto-publishing. The obvious last step is letting the bot schedule and post to LinkedIn. It won't. My profile is how founders and recruiters meet me. One invented detail, or one personal moment shared that I wasn't ready to share, costs more than every hour this saves.

Also cut: AI-generated visuals. My Voice Skill says real photos beat designed graphics, so the photo stays my job too.

05 · Stack

Cheap where it can be, careful where it counts

Scoring is mechanical, so it runs on a fast, cheap model. Drafting has to hold a voice across a whole post, so it gets the better writer.

CaptureTelegram Bot APIPrivate channel or DM. Text and voice notes.
HostingVercelOne serverless webhook plus this page.
Triage + keywordsGemini FlashFast, cheap, strict JSON scoring. Also transcribes voice notes.
News hookGoogle News RSSFree, no key. Headline, source, date and link, then a verify flag.
DraftingClaude (Gemini at L3)Reads the 20-section Raghav Voice Skill on every draft.
MemorySupabaseNotes, drafts and voice versions. Rejections are kept, never deleted.
Built withClaude Code + GitHubCode, version history and deploys.
